Rook Roll Dice Tower - Day 55 of 365
====================================

A castle dice tower with its rolling tray built on. Drop your dice in the
crenellated top: they bounce down three 45-degree baffles inside the tower
(you can watch them tumble past the arrow-slit windows) and roll out through
the pointed castle gate onto a felt-green tray. One solid print, no assembly.

Inspired by the dice towers that are always popular with tabletop gamers:
https://cults3d.com/en/search?q=dice+tower
Our twist: an original castle design with arrow-slit windows so you can watch
the roll, a corbelled crown, and a two-tone felt tray in one piece.

BAMBU LAB X2D + AMS (Bambu Studio)
----------------------------------
Open rook_roll_tower_X2D.3mf. One object, colours set:
  AMS slot 1  grey   #8A8F98  -> tower walls, baffles and tray
  AMS slot 2  dark   #2B2D33  -> crown with crenellations (top 16 mm)
  AMS slot 3  green  #2E7D4F  -> felt layer on the tray floor
PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no brim.
Print it standing up exactly as placed. All overhangs (baffle undersides,
crown corbel, gate arch) are 45 degrees and the window tops are small arches,
so it prints clean without supports. Colour changes are two whole bands
(felt floor at the bottom, crown at the top), so purge is low.
Roughly 215 g and 7 h (estimate, slice to confirm).
Size 152 x 90 x 160 mm.

SINGLE-COLOUR PRINTERS (e.g. Creality K1C)
------------------------------------------
Fits a 220 x 220 bed. Print one_colour.stl in any colour, or load
main_grey, crown_dark and felt_green together as one object and use a
filament swap at the layer where the crown starts (Z 142 mm).

DICE
----
Every dice path (top opening, between baffles, gate) is at least 24 mm,
so standard polyhedral sets including d20s roll through freely.
Tip: a thin felt sticker on the tray floor makes rolls quieter.

KEY PARAMETERS (in the source model)
wall 2.4, ramp_angle 45, tower_w 70, tower_h 160, tray_l 80, tray_w 90,
tray_h 20 (mm). STEP included to remix.
