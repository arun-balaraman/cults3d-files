Helix Marble Lift Tower - print settings and assembly
=====================================================
Turn the crank and a screw inside the tower lifts marbles up a groove. At the top
they roll out of the window onto the spiral track and run 3 turns back down to the
entry arch at the bottom, where the screw picks them up again.

Uses standard 16 mm glass marbles (5-7 at once works well).

Parts (all print in the orientation of the STL, no supports):
  tower_with_track.stl  96 x 96 x 214 mm  ~255 g  ~6.5 h  (track underside is 45 deg, self-supporting)
  lift_screw.stl        28 x 28 x 214 mm  ~26 g   ~1 h    (prints standing up; use a brim, 5 mm)
  top_cap.stl           47 x 47 x 12 mm   ~9 g    (flat top on the bed)
  crank.stl             58 x 20 x 34 mm   ~7 g    (arm flat on the bed)
Total about 300 g of PLA and about 8 h on a Creality K1C.

Settings: PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% gyroid infill, no supports.
Lift screw: 4 walls or 100% infill on the shaft for stiffness, brim on.
Lightly sand the inside of the groove and the track floor if marbles snag.

Assembly:
  1. Drop the lift screw into the tower bore, flat end first. The socket in its
     bottom sits on the pin in the tower floor.
  2. Slide the top cap over the screw shaft, spigot down, into the top of the tower.
  3. Push the crank onto the D-shaped shaft end (it only fits one way).
  4. Drop marbles into the entry arch at the bottom (or onto the track).
  5. Turn the crank CLOCKWISE (seen from above). About 6 turns lifts a marble to the top.

Clearances: 1 mm between screw flight and bore, 0.6 mm round each marble in the groove,
0.5 mm at the cap, 1 mm between crank arm and battlements.

Fresh design that hasn't been test-printed yet - please share a make!
