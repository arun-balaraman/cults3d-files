Floating Tensegrity Planter - print settings and assembly
=========================================================
A twisted octagon pot that seems to float above its plinth. The two crossing arms
never touch: one short string loop between them carries all the weight, and three
outer strings keep the tray level. It's a tensegrity structure.

Parts (print in the STL orientation, no supports):
  plinth.stl         104 x 104 x 85 mm  ~42 g  (base with the rising arm)
  floating_tray.stl   92 x 92 x 71 mm   ~31 g  (prints upside down: flat top on the bed, arm up)
  twisted_pot.stl     84 x 84 x 55 mm   ~45 g  (prints upright; vase-like twist)
Total about 120 g of PLA and about 4 h on a Creality K1C.

Settings: PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports. Brim optional for the two arm parts.
Silk or marble PLA looks great. The pot has no drainage hole: use it for a succulent in its
nursery pot, an air plant, a faux plant or as a desk tidy.

You also need about 1 m of strong thread: 0.5-0.8 mm braided fishing line is ideal.

Assembly:
  1. Central loop: thread the line through the small sideways hole in the plinth arm and the
     hole in the tray arm, and tie a loop so the two arms sit about 24 mm apart (hole to hole).
     The tray now hangs from the plinth arm.
  2. Outer strings: hold the tray level and centred, then run a string through each of the
     3 holes in the tray edge down to the matching hole in the plinth. Knot underneath (the
     plinth has knot recesses on its bottom). Pull all three snug and even.
  3. If the tray leans, tighten the string on the high side or loosen the low one.
  4. Set the pot on the engraved octagon outline on the tray.

Keep loads light (a small plant, under ~250 g).

Fresh design that hasn't been test-printed yet - please share a make!
