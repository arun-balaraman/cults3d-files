Petal-Grip Bathroom Caddy - Day 50 of 365 (designed in Autodesk Fusion)
=======================================================================

A toothbrush / bathroom caddy whose ports are flexible petal discs instead of
fixed holes. Push any handle in and the petals bend down and hug it, so thin
razors, chunky electric-brush handles and toothpaste tubes all stand upright.

PRIMARY: Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro (Bambu Studio)
-------------------------------------------------
Open petal_grip_caddy_X2D.3mf. Caddy + drip tray on one plate, colours set:
  AMS slot 1  white     #F6F6F2  -> caddy body
  AMS slot 2  teal      #2BB3A3  -> 3 small petal ports (first 6 layers only)
  AMS slot 3  coral     #FF7A59  -> large petal port (first 6 layers only)
  AMS slot 4  charcoal  #2D2F34  -> drip tray
Material: PETG recommended for a bathroom (springier petals, water-proof);
PLA works too. Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no brim.
Orientation is already set: the caddy prints upside-down, top deck on the
bed, so the petals are the first 1.2 mm and come out flat and smooth. The
colour change only happens in the first 6 layers, so purge waste is small.
Roughly 150 g and 4.5 h on the X2D (estimate, slice to confirm).
Tip: a textured PEI plate gives the top deck a nice matte finish.

SECONDARY: Creality K1C (single colour)
---------------------------------------
Load caddy_white.stl, petals_teal.stl and petals_coral.stl together as one
object (they line up) and print in one colour, or pause for a filament change
at Z 1.2 mm to get coloured petals. Print tray_charcoal.stl separately.
Both parts fit the 220 x 220 bed.

USE
---
1. Set the tray down, stand the caddy in it (it sits on ribs so water drains
   off and air dries the bottom).
2. Push handles straight down through the petals. Small ports: toothbrushes,
   razors, pens (about 6-22 mm). Large port: toothpaste tube cap-down,
   electric brush handle, hairbrush (up to about 34 mm).
3. Lift the caddy off the tray to rinse it.

KEY DIMENSIONS (user parameters in petal_grip_caddy.f3d)
body_h 100, wall 2.4, petal_t 1.2, slit_w 0.8, ledge 1.0, tray_clear 3 (mm).
Small ports 24 mm with 8 petals, large port 36 mm with 12 petals. Thinner
petal_t = softer grip. STEP included to remix (add or move ports).

Fresh design, not test printed yet. Please post a make if you try it!
