Helix Drop Spinner - Day 47 of 365 (designed in Autodesk Fusion)
================================================================

PRIMARY: Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro (Bambu Studio)
-------------------------------------------------
Open helix_drop_spinner_X2D.3mf. Three objects on one plate, colours assigned:
  AMS slot 1  teal   #148C96  -> stand base + cap
  AMS slot 2  white  #F5F5F0  -> twisted helix bar + disc
  AMS slot 3  black  #19191C  -> Benham pattern (1 mm inlay, first 5 layers
                                 of the disc only)
Material: PLA. Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no brim
(add a small brim on the stand if your bed adhesion is weak: the bar is tall).
Orientation is already set: stand upright (base on the bed, bar up), cap
top-face-down, disc top-face-down (pattern on the bed).
Roughly 65 g and 3 h on the X2D (estimate, slice to confirm).
The colour change on the stand is a single swap at Z 10 mm (base -> bar).

SECONDARY: Creality K1C (single colour)
---------------------------------------
stand_base_teal.stl + stand_helix_white.stl together (one stand),
cap_teal.stl and rider_white.stl. Skip rider_pattern_black.stl (the disc is
then plain) or do a filament change at Z 1.0 mm on the disc.
Fits the 220 x 220 bed.

ASSEMBLY
--------
1. Slide the disc onto the twisted bar from the top, pattern side up. It
   should corkscrew down freely. If it rubs, sand the bar edges lightly.
2. Press the cap onto the square peg at the top of the bar (5 mm peg,
   5.3 mm socket). A drop of glue is optional if your printer runs loose.
3. Play: flip the whole thing over like an hourglass. The disc spins its way
   down the helix. Watch the black-and-white Benham pattern: many people see
   faint coloured rings while it spins (works best under bright LED light).

KEY DIMENSIONS (user parameters in helix_drop_spinner.f3d)
base_d 80, bar_len 130, twist 540 deg, bar_w 14, bar_t 4.5,
slide_gap 0.6 per side, rider_d 66, peg_fit 0.3 (mm).
STEP included to remix (e.g. a longer bar or more twist).
