Reindeer Shuffle - print-in-place sliding puzzle ornament, one piece, one colour (Creality K1C, 0.4 mm nozzle)

File: reindeer_shuffle.stl  (61.6 x 71.6 x 9.0 mm: frame + 8 tiles already in place)
Place it flat exactly as it comes (no rotating, no scaling).

Material:     PLA (red, green, white or silk all look good)
Layer height: 0.2 mm (first layer 0.2 mm)
Walls:        3 perimeters
Top/bottom:   4 / 4 layers
Infill:       15% gyroid
Supports:     OFF
Brim:         OFF (a brim would glue the tiles to the frame at the bed)
Elephant-foot compensation: 0.15 mm
Seam:         aligned or rear
Ironing:      optional, top surfaces only (makes the raised picture crisper)

Do not scale: the 0.6 mm gaps only work at 100%.

After printing: let it cool fully. Hold the frame and push the tile next to the
empty corner sideways into the gap with your thumb, then push it back. Do this
for each tile next to the gap, then shuffle a few times. The first moves feel
stiff while the thin skin at the bed breaks; after that the tiles glide.
If one tile is stuck, press on it from underneath while sliding it.
