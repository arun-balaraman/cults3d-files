Jingle Loop - print-in-place endless bell chain, one piece, one colour (Creality K1C, 0.4 mm nozzle)

File: jingle_loop.stl  (88.0 x 58.0 x 8.8 mm: frame + island + 12 linked bells already in place)
Place it flat exactly as it comes (no rotating, no scaling).

Material:     PLA (green, red, gold or silk all look good)
Layer height: 0.2 mm (first layer 0.2 mm)
Walls:        3 perimeters
Top/bottom:   4 / 4 layers
Infill:       15% gyroid
Supports:     OFF
Brim:         OFF (a brim would glue the bells to the frame at the bed)
Elephant-foot compensation: 0.15 mm
Seam:         aligned or rear
Speed:        normal K1C PLA profile; outer wall <= 200 mm/s

Do not scale: the 0.6 mm gaps only work at 100%.

After printing: let it cool fully. Hold the frame flat, press a thumb on one bell
and push it a few millimetres along the track, then back. Do this at 3 or 4 places
round the loop so every bell breaks free from the bed, then push the whole chain
round a few full laps both ways. The first lap feels stiff; after that it glides.
If the island sticks, press it down from underneath while pushing the bells.
