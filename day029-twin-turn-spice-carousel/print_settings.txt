Twin-Turn Spice Carousel - print settings (Creality K1C or any 220 x 220 mm printer)

All parts: PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% gyroid infill, NO supports, no brim needed
(brim optional on the base halves). Print every part flat side down exactly as the STL is oriented.

File                          Qty  Size (mm)          Est. filament / time each
base_half_print_x2.stl        2    185 x 186 x 24     ~110 g / ~3.5 h   (same file twice)
turntable_print_x2.stl        2    155 x 155 x 18     ~70 g  / ~2.5 h
roller_cage_print_x2.stl      2    132 x 132 x 3      ~30 g  / ~1 h
rollers_12_print_x2.stl       2    47 x 34 x 14       ~9 g   / ~40 min  (12 rollers per plate, 24 total)
keys_set_print_x1.stl         1    46 x 44 x 24       ~6 g   / ~20 min  (3 floor keys + 2 wall keys)
Total about 440 g and ~16 h of printing, spread over 9 plates.

Fit: 0.6 mm post/bore clearance, 0.6 mm gear backlash (module 3, 50 teeth, 20 deg involute), 0.2 mm key clearance.
If your printer runs tight, scale ONLY the keys to 98%.

ASSEMBLY (no glue, no screws, no metal)
1. Put the two base halves end to end on the table, cut faces touching.
2. Drop the 3 small bow-tie keys into the floor slots along the join (y = 0 and +-45 mm).
3. Slide the 2 tall bow-tie keys down into the slots in the side bosses. The tray is now one piece.
4. Lay 12 rollers in the pockets of each cage, then lower each cage over a centre post; it rests on the post shoulder.
5. Lower each turntable over its post so it sits on the rollers; turn one slightly so the teeth drop into mesh.
6. Spin either turntable: both turn (in opposite directions). Lift a turntable straight up to clean underneath.
