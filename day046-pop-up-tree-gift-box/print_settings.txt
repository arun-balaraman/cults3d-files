Pop-Up Tree Gift Box - Day 46 of 365 (designed in Autodesk Fusion)
===================================================================

PRIMARY: Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro (Bambu Studio)
-------------------------------------------------
Open pop_up_tree_gift_box_X2D.3mf. Everything is on one plate, colours assigned:
  AMS slot 1  red    #C8202A  -> gift box + lid
  AMS slot 2  white  #F5F5F0  -> ribbon inlays (1 mm, on box sides and lid)
  AMS slot 3  green  #1E8C46  -> 3-tier tree (prints in place INSIDE the box)
  AMS slot 4  gold   #E6B428  -> star
Material: PLA. Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 15% infill, NO supports, no brim.
Orientation is already set: box upright with the tree inside it (print in
place), lid top-face-down (ribbon cross on the bed), star flat.
About 165 g and roughly 6 h on the X2D (estimate, slice to confirm).
Keep "Avoid crossing walls" on and do not add supports: the gaps between the
box and the tree (1.0 mm) and between tiers (1.8 mm+) must stay clear.

SECONDARY: Creality K1C (single colour)
---------------------------------------
gift_box_red.stl + tree_green_print_in_place.stl together (they are already
positioned to print in place), lid_red.stl and star_gold.stl separately.
Skip the ribbon files (the box/lid simply stay plain) or print them as a
separate colour. Fits the 220 x 220 bed.

AFTER PRINTING
--------------
1. Take the box off the bed. Turn it over and give the tree a gentle push from
   underneath (the box is an open sleeve) to free the tiers.
2. Press the star onto the square post on top of the tree.
3. Pull the star up: each tier slides out and clicks onto a small wedge at
   full height. Tree top ends about 210 mm above the table.
4. Push down firmly on the star to collapse it back, then put the lid on.
   You can hide a gift card, money roll or small present on top of the tree.

KEY DIMENSIONS (listed as user parameters in pop_up_tree_gift_box.f3d)
box_w 80, box_h 78, wall 2.4, tier_h 42, tier_wall 1.6, slide_gap 0.8,
wedge 0.25, lid_gap 0.4, ribbon_w 12 (mm). STEP included to remix.
If the click is too stiff on your printer, sand the small lock wedges lightly.

Fresh design, not test printed yet. Please post a make if you try it!
