Gimbal Star - print settings
============================
Parts:
  gimbal_print_in_place.stl  five nested rings + star disc, printed in place, flat on the bed  ~41 g, ~1.5 h
  stand.stl                  prints as exported (base down)                                 ~27 g, ~1 h
Material: PLA (silk gold/rainbow looks fantastic), 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, NO supports.
Print the gimbal at normal speed with good cooling; do not use a brim (it can glue the rings together).

Freeing it: when the print is cool, hold the outer ring and gently twist each inner ring until the tiny
pivot pins break free (0.6 mm clearance all round). Work from the outside in. A first "crack" is normal.
The pins are short horizontal cones, the same trick most print-in-place gimbals use; they print fine at
0.2 mm with cooling on.

Display: drop the two knobs on the outer ring into the V notches on the stand. Flick any ring and the whole
thing tumbles; each ring pivots at right angles to the one around it.
