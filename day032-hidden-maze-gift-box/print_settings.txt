Hidden Maze Gift Box - print settings (Creality K1C or any printer)

PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls (the walls are thin, so this is nearly solid), 15% infill, NO supports.
maze_cup.stl  - prints upright on its gripped flange.            ~33 g, ~1 h 40 min
maze_lid.stl  - prints upside down (cap on the bed), pins at top. ~30 g, ~1 h 20 min
Print at 100% scale. Elephant-foot compensation 0.15 mm helps the lid slide over the flange.
Seam position: "aligned" or "back" so the seam doesn't land on the pins.

Use: slide the lid down over the cup so the two pins drop into the entry slots at the rim, then twist and
push/lift to follow the maze down to the bottom ("home"); the lid then sits on the flange and can't be
lifted off. To open, find the way back out. The maze is the same on both sides (one per pin).
The solution is in solution_spoiler.png. Gaps: 0.4 mm lid to cup, ~0.55 mm pins to maze walls.
If it feels tight, sand the pins lightly; don't scale the parts.
