Peek-a-Boo Cat Bookends - Day 56 of 365
=======================================

A pair of low-poly ginger cat bookends. The head end has the cat's front
half (face, white bib and paws) and the tail end has its back half with a
curled, white-tipped tail. Put a row of books between them and the cat looks
like it is walking behind your books. Each end is one print with the
colours inlaid, no supports and no glue.

Inspired by the low-poly papercraft cats going viral right now:
https://cults3d.com/en/search?q=low+poly+cat
Our twist: the cat is split across two bookends so it seems to pass through
your book row, and every colour is a flush inlay, so it prints in one go.

BAMBU LAB X2D + AMS (Bambu Studio)
----------------------------------
Open peekaboo_cat_bookends_X2D.3mf. Both ends are already placed on one
256 mm plate with colours set:
  AMS slot 1  charcoal #2E3034 -> plates and eyes
  AMS slot 2  ginger   #E2782A -> cat body
  AMS slot 3  cream    #F3E6C8 -> muzzle, bib, paws, tail tip
  AMS slot 4  pink     #F08CA0 -> nose and inner ears (head end only)
PLA (PETG works too), 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% gyroid infill,
no supports, no brim needed (the base is 110 mm wide and flat).
Print them standing exactly as placed. Every overhang on the cat is
45 degrees or steeper, and the inlays are 1.6 mm deep flush panels.
Tip: turn on "flush into infill" to cut purge waste.
Roughly 420 g for the pair plus purge, about 15 h (estimate, slice to confirm).
You can also print one end per plate with the same file.

SINGLE-COLOUR PRINTERS (e.g. Creality K1C)
------------------------------------------
Each end is 173 x 110 x 170 mm and fits a 220 x 220 bed one at a time.
Print one_colour_head.stl and one_colour_tail.stl in any colour.
The separate colour STLs (head_1..4, tail_1..3) share the same origin,
so you can also load them together as one object in any multi-colour slicer.

USING THEM
----------
- The 100 mm plate slides under the books; their weight holds the bookend.
- Six 10 mm recesses underneath take standard self-adhesive rubber bumpers
  for grip on smooth shelves (optional).
- The upright is 6 mm thick with the cat body bracing it on the outside.
- Tail end goes on the left, head end on the right, both plates pointing
  in under the books. The cat then faces you as it walks to the right.

KEY PARAMETERS (in the source model)
upright_t 6, base_t 3.2, book_depth 100, pad_d 10.4 (mm). STEP included to remix.
