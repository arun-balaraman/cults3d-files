Push-Bloom Lily - print settings and assembly (Creality K1C or any printer)

PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20% infill, NO supports. Print each file as oriented.
stem.stl               upright (flange on the bed), 113 mm tall      ~22 g / 70 min
plunger.stl            disc on the bed, rod pointing up               ~9 g  / 25 min  (slow down or print with the stem for cooling)
petals_x6.stl          all six petals, flat side down, tails up       ~11 g / 35 min  (pink!)
flower_centre_cap.stl  engraved face on the bed                        ~1 g  / 6 min   (yellow)
button.stl             flat on the bed                                  ~2 g  / 8 min
Total about 45 g and 2.5 h. Pause-free colour: print petals in pink, cap in yellow, the rest in green.

Clearances: 0.4 mm rod in the stem, 0.35 mm knob in the plunger head, axle 1.2 mm radius in a 1.6 mm seat.

ASSEMBLY (no glue)
1. Slide the plunger rod down through the top of the stem until the slotted disc sits in the cup.
2. Press the button onto the rod end under the stem (tight press fit; a drop of glue is optional).
3. Pull the button down (bud closed). Hold a petal upright, tail pointing inward, lay its knob on the
   slotted disc either side of a slot, and press its axle down into the snap slots in the cup until it clicks.
   Repeat for all six petals.
4. Press the yellow centre cap's peg into the hole in the middle of the plunger disc. The knobs are now
   trapped between the two slotted discs.
5. Push the button: the lily opens. Pull it: it closes.
If a petal snap is too tight, shave the slot lips with a knife; if too loose, a dab of glue on the post tops.
