Facet Cat Stash Box - Day 51 of 365 (designed in Autodesk Fusion)
=================================================================

A low-poly "papercraft" ginger cat that is secretly a stash box. The head
twist-locks onto the body with a 3-lug bayonet: no threads, no magnets.

Inspired by the viral Meimei Cat Papercraft (top free download on Cults3D):
https://cults3d.com/en/search?q=Meimei+Cat+Papercraft
Our twist: no paper, no glue. It is a solid printed faceted cat, and the head
twists off to reveal a hidden compartment inside the body.

PRIMARY: Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro (Bambu Studio)
-------------------------------------------------
Open facet_cat_stash_X2D.3mf. Body and head on one plate, colours set:
  AMS slot 1  orange  #F08A24  -> body and head
  AMS slot 2  green   #7BD389  -> eye inlays
  AMS slot 3  pink    #F49AC1  -> nose and inner-ear inlays
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no brim.
Both parts print upright as placed (body open end up, head on its flat neck;
the socket ceiling is a 45 degree cone so it needs no support).
The inlays span many layers on the head, so keep the prime tower on (default)
or use "flush into infill" to cut purge waste.
Roughly 110 g and 4 h on the X2D (estimate, slice to confirm).
Assembled size about 93 x 97 x 130 mm.

SECONDARY: Creality K1C (single colour)
---------------------------------------
Print body_orange.stl and head_orange.stl (both fit the 220 x 220 bed).
The inlay STLs are optional: load them with the head as one object for a
single-colour print, or paint the eyes/nose afterwards.

OPEN / CLOSE
------------
Close: set the head on the neck with its face turned about 40 degrees to the
cat's left, push down, then twist clockwise until it stops. The face now
points forward and the head is locked.
Open: twist the head anticlockwise about 40 degrees and lift.

KEY DIMENSIONS (user parameters in facet_cat_stash.f3d)
wall 2.4, tube_r 16.7, bore_r 17.0, lug_gap 0.6, inlay 1.2 (mm).
If your printer runs tight, raise bore_r by 0.2. Stash opening 28.6 mm wide.
STEP included to remix.
