Channel-Flip TV Lamp - Day 49 of 365 (designed in Autodesk Fusion)
==================================================================

A retro TV night-light. The white drum inside is a 4-sided lithophane with a
scene on each face (mountains + moon, sailboat, rocket + stars, house + tree).
Turn the knob on top and the drum turns: a new "channel" shows in the screen.

USE AN LED TEA LIGHT ONLY. Never a real flame: PLA softens and burns.

PRIMARY: Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro (Bambu Studio)
-------------------------------------------------
Open channel_flip_tv_lamp_X2D.3mf. All 5 parts on one plate, colours set:
  AMS slot 1  mint      #78C8B2  -> TV cabinet
  AMS slot 2  white     #F5F5F0  -> channel drum (use plain white PLA, not
                                    silk or translucent, for the best scenes)
  AMS slot 3  charcoal  #2D2F34  -> base, channel knob, antenna, 2 dial knobs
Material: PLA. Layer height 0.16 mm, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports.
Per-object setting for the drum: 100% infill (the 0.8 mm windows and 2.8 mm
silhouettes then print solid, which is what makes the lithophane work).
Orientation is already set:
  - cabinet upside-down (roof on the bed, open bottom up). The bottom edge of
    the screen opening becomes a ~38 mm bridge; Bambu bridges this fine.
  - drum upright (open end on the bed) so the picture walls stand vertical.
    Its top plate is a 50 mm bridge, hidden inside the TV.
  - knob top-down, antenna hub-down, base flat.
Roughly 210 g and 7.5 h on the X2D (estimate, slice to confirm).

SECONDARY: Creality K1C (single colour)
---------------------------------------
Print each STL separately in its own colour (or print everything in one colour
and paint the knobs). Every part fits the 220 x 220 bed. Keep the drum in white.
  cabinet_mint.stl, dial_knobs_charcoal.stl, base_charcoal.stl,
  drum_white.stl, knob_charcoal.stl, antenna_charcoal.stl
The two dial knobs are separate on the K1C: glue them onto the front face
(above and below the grille) if you print the cabinet in one colour.

ASSEMBLY (no glue on the X2D version)
-------------------------------------
1. Put an LED tea light (38 mm) in the round pocket of the base.
2. Stand the drum over it, open end down. Its bottom rim drops around the
   centring ring so it spins on the spot.
3. Lower the cabinet over the drum onto the base. Two rails and a front lip
   on the base locate it.
4. Push the knob's hex shaft through the hole in the roof into the hex socket
   on top of the drum.
5. Sit the antenna hub in the round recess on the roof.
6. Switch the tea light on, turn the knob a quarter turn per channel.

KEY DIMENSIONS (user parameters in channel_flip_tv_lamp.f3d)
Cabinet 110 x 96 x 84 mm, wall 2.6. Drum 56 x 56 x 62 mm with 0.8 mm picture
walls and 2.8 mm silhouettes. Hex drive 10 mm across flats in a 10.3 socket.
Overall 116 x 102 x 147 mm with antenna. STEP included to remix: swap in your
own scenes on the inside faces of the drum.

Fresh design, not test printed yet. Please post a make if you try it!
