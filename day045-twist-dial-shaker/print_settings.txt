Twist-Dial Spice Shaker - Day 45 of 365 (designed in Autodesk Fusion)
=====================================================================

PRIMARY: Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro (Bambu Studio)
-------------------------------------------------
Open twist_dial_shaker_X2D.3mf. Everything is on one plate, colours assigned:
  AMS slot 1  white      #F4F1EA  -> jar + lid
  AMS slot 2 terracotta #D2553A  -> dial
  AMS slot 3  charcoal   #2B2B2B  -> position icons (1 mm inlay in the lid top,
                                     only the first 5 layers of the lid)
Material: PLA (PETG is better for a jar that gets washed). Food note: use a
food-safe filament and treat printed jars as dry-spice storage, hand wash only.
Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no brim.
Orientation is already set: jar upright, lid TOP-FACE-DOWN (icons on the bed),
dial TOP-FACE-DOWN with its snap post pointing up.
About 70 g and 2.5 h on the X2D.

SECONDARY: Creality K1C (single colour)
---------------------------------------
Use jar_white.stl, lid_white.stl and dial_terracotta.stl (fits the 220 x 220 bed).
Skip icons_charcoal.stl (the icon pockets just stay as 1 mm recesses), or do a
filament change at Z 1.0 mm on the lid. Same settings, about 3 h.

ASSEMBLY
--------
1. Push the dial's split post down through the centre hole of the lid until it
   clicks under the lid plate. It should turn with a click at each position.
2. Fill the jar, screw the lid on (1.5 turns).
3. Positions (clockwise): X closed, 3 small dots fine, 2 big dots coarse,
   oval = pour spout.

FUSION PARAMETERS (Modify > Change Parameters in twist_dial_shaker.f3d)
jar_d 62, jar_h 95, wall 2, thread_pitch 2.5, thread_clear 0.4,
hole_small 2, hole_large 4, dial_clear 0.3, detent_h 0.5 (mm).
If the lid is tight on your printer, open thread_clear slightly.
