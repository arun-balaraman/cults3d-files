Crack-Jaw Nutcracker Soldier - Day 53 of 365
============================================

A classic Christmas nutcracker soldier whose jaw really works. The white
beard is a lever: it pivots on a pin through the head, and its handle runs
down his back. Lift the handle to open the mouth, drop a nut in, press the
handle down to close the jaw. Three parts, no glue.

Inspired by the nutcracker designs trending on Cults3D this Christmas
(for example the Nutcracker christmas mask): https://cults3d.com/en/search?q=nutcracker
Our twist: an original soldier with a working lever jaw, printed in 4 colours.

BAMBU LAB X2D + AMS (Bambu Studio)
----------------------------------
Open crack_jaw_nutcracker_X2D.3mf. All three parts on one plate, colours set:
  AMS slot 1  red    #C81E28  -> jacket and arms
  AMS slot 2  black  #232328  -> base, boots, belt, hat, eyes, pivot pin
  AMS slot 3  white  #F5F5F0  -> trousers, buttons, moustache, hair, beard lever
  AMS slot 4  skin   #F2C8A0  -> neck and face
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no brim.
Orientation is already set: the soldier stands upright, the beard lever lies
flat on its side, the pin stands on its cap. Colour changes are mostly whole
height bands (boots, trousers, jacket, face, hat), so purge is moderate.
Roughly 110 g and 6.5 h (estimate, slice to confirm).
Assembled size 68 x 68 x 181 mm.

SINGLE-COLOUR PRINTERS (e.g. Creality K1C)
------------------------------------------
Every part fits a 220 x 220 bed. Load body_red, body_black, body_white and
body_skin together as one object (they line up) and print in one colour, or
paint it afterwards. Print jaw_white.stl and pin_black.stl separately.

ASSEMBLY
--------
1. Slide the beard lever into the slot in the head from behind, beard to the
   front, handle hanging down his back.
2. Line the hole in the lever up with the holes in the head and push the pin
   in from his right side (the side with the recessed hole). The far hole is
   a press fit, so push firmly until the cap sits in its recess.
3. Lift the handle to open the jaw, drop in a nut, and press the handle down.
   Best with soft nuts (peanuts, pistachios); hard walnuts may be too much for
   plastic. Also makes a nice decoration.

KEY PARAMETERS (in the source model)
jaw_gap 0.6 (lever side clearance), pin_r 3.0, pin_free 0.3 (lever hole
clearance), pin_press 0.1 (far-side press fit), mouth 8.5 (mm).
