Tally Click - Day 57 of 365
===========================

A pocket clicker fidget that keeps count. Every press of the coral button
gives a crisp click and rolls the number drum on by one, so the digit in the
window counts 0, 1, 2 ... 9 and then starts again. Use it as a fidget, a
lap counter, a push-up or rep counter, or a tally for anything.
Four printed parts plus one small rubber band. No glue, no screws.

Inspired by the rubber-band clicker fidgets that are among the most searched
and printed designs right now: https://cults3d.com/en/search?q=clicker+fidget
Our twist: the clicker is also a counter. A ratchet and pawl turns a
ten-sided drum with seven-segment digits, and a sprung detent snaps each
number square in the window.

BAMBU LAB X2D + AMS (Bambu Studio)
----------------------------------
Open tally_click_X2D.3mf. All four parts are placed on one 256 mm plate,
already in print orientation, with colours set:
  AMS slot 1  teal     #1F7A8C -> case and lid
  AMS slot 2  coral    #E8553D -> plunger (button)
  AMS slot 3  white    #F2F2EE -> number drum
  AMS slot 4  charcoal #2B2D33 -> digits (inlaid into the drum)
PLA, 0.16 mm layers (0.2 mm is fine for the case and lid), 3 walls,
20% infill, no supports, no brim. Every part prints flat as placed:
  case on its back with the open side up, lid flat, plunger on its side,
  drum standing on its plain end with the ratchet teeth on top.
PETG for the plunger is best for long life because its pawl flexes on every
click (PLA works too). If you want PETG, print the plunger on its own plate.
About 32 g of filament and roughly 2 h with colour changes (estimate, slice
to confirm). Tip: turn on "flush into infill" to cut purge waste.

SINGLE-COLOUR PRINTERS (e.g. Creality K1C)
------------------------------------------
Everything fits a 220 x 220 bed. Print case_teal.stl, lid_teal.stl,
plunger_coral.stl and drum_white.stl in any colour(s). The digits on
drum_white.stl are recessed 0.8 mm, so colour them in with a paint marker
or fill them with a little acrylic paint and wipe the face.
digits_charcoal.stl and drum_white.stl share the same origin, so any
multi-colour slicer can load them together as one object.

WHAT ELSE YOU NEED
------------------
One small rubber band for the return spring: a standard loom band
(the kids' bracelet kind) is perfect. A small hair elastic or a
6 mm (1/4 inch) orthodontic elastic also works. The band stretches
between two posts, 22 mm apart at rest and 27 mm when pressed.

ASSEMBLY (about 2 minutes)
--------------------------
1. Push the drum onto the round axle inside the case, ratchet teeth
   towards the open side. Turn it so the 0 shows in the window; it clicks
   into place on each number.
2. Loop the rubber band over the post at the bottom of the plunger.
3. Feed the plunger in from the open side so its stem comes up through
   the slot in the top. The thin pawl strip sits just above the
   ratchet teeth.
4. Hook the free end of the band over the post inside the case (upper back
   corner). The button should now spring up on its own.
5. Slide the lid into the grooves from the bottom and push it up until the
   latch clicks. To open it again, press the narrow tab at the bottom back
   corner of the lid towards the front and slide the lid back down.
Press the button: click, and the number goes up by one.

KEY PARAMETERS (in the source model)
stroke 5, pawl 1.4 thick, 10 ratchet teeth, detent preload 0.3,
digit inlay 0.8 deep, lid clearance 0.15 per side, axle clearance 0.3,
button slot clearance 0.4 (mm). STEP included to remix.
