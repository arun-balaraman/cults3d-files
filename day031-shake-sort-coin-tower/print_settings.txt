Shake-Sort Coin Tower (Australian coins) - print settings (Creality K1C or any 120 x 120 mm+ bed)

PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, NO supports, one part per plate, as oriented in the STL.
Turn elephant-foot compensation ON (0.15 mm) and keep horizontal hole expansion at 0 so the hole sizes stay true.

File                   Keeps   Hole dia   ~g / ~time
tray1_top_50c.stl      50c     30.2 mm    28 g / 45 min
tray2_20c.stl          20c     26.8 mm    30 g / 45 min
tray3_1dollar.stl      $1      24.3 mm    30 g / 45 min
tray4_10c.stl          10c     22.0 mm    29 g / 45 min
tray5_2dollar.stl      $2      19.95 mm   30 g / 45 min
tray6_bottom_5c.stl    5c      (solid)    35 g / 45 min
lid.stl  (prints top face down, "SHAKE" reads correctly when flipped)  35 g / 40 min
Total about 200 g and 5.5 h.

Stack from the bottom up: 5c, $2, 10c, $1, 20c, 50c, lid (the labels on the front go 50c..5c top to bottom).
Pour coins into the top tray, put the lid on, hold the tower with both hands and shake side to side for ~10 s.
Tightest step is $2 (20.5 mm) vs 5c (19.4 mm): if a 5c sticks in the $2 tray, check that your $2 tray holes
measure ~19.9 mm; if a $2 slips through, scale ONLY tray5 to 99%.
