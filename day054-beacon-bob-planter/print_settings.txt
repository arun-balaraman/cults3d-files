Beacon Bob Self-Watering Planter - Day 54 of 365
================================================

A self-watering planter with a lighthouse water gauge. The pot sits in a
water tank, and a slotted wick foot under the pot reaches down into the water
so the soil drinks as it needs to. The lighthouse is joined to the tank at the
bottom, so the water level inside the tower matches the tank. A hollow float
inside the tower carries the yellow beacon: when the tank is full the beacon
rises about 30 mm out of the red gallery, and as the water runs low it sinks
back down. Top up by pouring water into the gallery at the top of the tower.

Inspired by the self-watering planters that are always popular on Cults3D:
https://cults3d.com/en/search?q=self+watering+planter
Our twist: an original lighthouse design whose beacon shows the water level.

BAMBU LAB X2D + AMS (Bambu Studio)
----------------------------------
Open beacon_bob_planter_X2D.3mf. All three prints on one plate, colours set:
  AMS slot 1  blue    #2E6DA4  -> tank
  AMS slot 2  white   #F5F5F0  -> surf crest, lighthouse tower, pot
  AMS slot 3  red     #C81E28  -> tower bands and gallery
  AMS slot 4  yellow  #F5C518  -> beacon float
PETG recommended for the tank (holds water better than PLA), 0.2 mm layers,
4 walls, 5 bottom layers, 15% infill, no supports.
Beacon float: print upright as placed, 2 walls, 0-15% infill and a brim
(it is tall and thin). It has sealed hollows built in so it floats.
Orientation is already set: tank and pot stand upright, the float stands on
its round end. Roughly 300 g and 12 h for the whole plate (estimate, slice to
confirm). Print the parts on separate plates if you prefer shorter jobs.
Assembled size 148 x 119 x 165 mm. Pot holds a plant up to about 100 mm wide.

SINGLE-COLOUR PRINTERS (e.g. Creality K1C)
------------------------------------------
Every part fits a 220 x 220 bed. Print base_one_colour.stl (tank and
lighthouse as one piece), pot_white.stl and beacon_float_yellow.stl in any
colours you like.

ASSEMBLY AND USE
----------------
1. Drop the float into the top of the lighthouse, round end first. It should
   slide freely; if it sticks, sand the float lightly.
2. Fill the pot with potting mix, pressing some into the slotted wick foot,
   and plant. Lower the pot into the tank with the notch in its rim against
   the lighthouse.
3. Pour water into the gallery at the top of the lighthouse until the beacon
   rises. When the beacon has sunk back into the gallery, it is time to top up.
4. Water from the top once at planting so the wick foot gets going.
Tip: test the tank with water before planting. A coat of clear sealer inside
helps if a print weeps.

KEY PARAMETERS (in the source model)
bore_r 11 (tower bore radius), bob_r 10 (float radius), wall 2.4 (tank and
pot walls), travel 30 (beacon rise from empty to full), all in mm.
