Dig-Dock Excavator Phone Stand - Day 52 of 365 (designed in Autodesk Fusion)
============================================================================

A desk excavator that is also a phone stand. Your phone stands in the bucket
and leans back on the arm at about 72 degrees, and the upper body swivels on
the tracks via a snap-on pin. Three parts, no glue, no hardware.

Inspired by the CT132 Mobile Excavator 1:14, one of the most-liked new models
on Cults3D this fortnight: https://cults3d.com/en/search?q=CT132+Mobile+Excavator
Our twist: not a scale model but a small desk toy that does a job. The bucket
is a phone cradle, the arm is the backrest, and the cab swivels.

PRIMARY: Bambu Lab X2D + AMS 2 Pro (Bambu Studio)
-------------------------------------------------
Open dig_dock_excavator_X2D.3mf. All three parts on one plate, colours set:
  AMS slot 1  yellow    #F7BA1E  -> arm and upper body
  AMS slot 2  charcoal  #303238  -> track base
  AMS slot 3  blue      #78BEEB  -> cab windows (inlays, 13 mm band only)
PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no brim.
Orientation is already set:
  - base flat on its tracks, swivel pin pointing up
  - upper body standing on its bottom (the pin socket is a plain bore with a
    45 degree roof on the snap groove, so it needs no support)
  - arm lying on its side (it is a flat profile, 24 mm thick; the dovetail
    flanks are 45 degrees)
Each part is a single colour except the body, where blue only appears in the
window band, so purge waste is small.
Roughly 130 g and 4.5 h on the X2D (estimate, slice to confirm).
Assembled size 167 x 78 x 132 mm.

SECONDARY: Creality K1C (single colour)
---------------------------------------
All parts fit the 220 x 220 bed. Print base_charcoal.stl, body_yellow.stl and
arm_yellow.stl. windows_blue.stl is optional: load it with the body as one
object for single colour, or pause at Z 25 and Z 38 for a colour swap.

ASSEMBLY
--------
1. Push the upper body straight down onto the pin in the middle of the tracks
   until it clicks (the split pin squeezes and the ridge snaps into a groove).
   It now swivels freely but won't lift off.
2. Slide the arm's dovetail down into the slot on the front of the body until
   it stops. The bucket should just touch the desk.
3. Stand your phone in the bucket and lean it back on the arm. The bucket slot
   is 13 mm wide, so most phones fit with a case on.

KEY DIMENSIONS (user parameters in dig_dock_excavator.f3d)
pin_r 8.0, pin_gap 0.4, snap 0.8, dove_gap 0.25, slot_w 13, lip_h 13 (mm),
lean 72 deg. If the arm slides in too tight, sand the dovetail lightly or
reprint it with a larger dove_gap. STEP included to remix.

Fresh design, not test printed yet. Please post a make if you try it!
