Battery Waterfall - print settings
==================================
A gravity battery dispenser for AA and AAA cells. Drop batteries in the slots at the
top: they roll down the zig-zag ramps and queue up. Take one from the pocket at the
bottom and every battery above rolls down one place.

Holds about 25 AA (left) and 33 AAA (right).
Size 149 x 159 x 60 mm. One piece.

Print: lying on its BACK (as the STL is oriented), no supports.
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill. About 230 g and 5 h on a Creality K1C
(the ramps and walls are thin, so most of it prints as solid perimeters).
Ramp windows have pointed tops and the front lips are chamfered, so nothing bridges.

Use: stand it on a desk or shelf, or hang it on the wall with 2 screws through the
keyholes in the back (screw heads up to 8 mm).
Load batteries through the slots in the top, flat end first, any way round.
Ramps slope 8 degrees; if a battery hesitates, a light sand of the ramp tops helps.

Fresh design that hasn't been test-printed yet - please share a make!
